10 FAQ

The details, clarified.

Do you replace our existing project manager?

Not necessarily. We can work alongside your existing PM, operations manager or account manager to provide additional coordination and execution capacity.

What tools do you work with?

We can work with your existing systems, including ClickUp, Asana, Monday.com, Trello, Airtable and Basecamp.

Do you work with remote teams?

Yes. Our workflows are designed for remote and distributed teams.

Can you manage contractors and freelancers?

Yes. We can coordinate internal employees, freelancers, agencies and external contractors as part of the project workflow.

Do you only manage digital projects?

Digital projects are one of our core specialties, but our project management and operations services can be adapted to other types of business projects.

Do I need to hire you full-time?

No. Our fractional model allows you to get project management support based on your actual workload.
